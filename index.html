DailyMint

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026
Last updated: October 5, 2026

In short: DailyMint is designed to keep your financial information on your device. The app does not require an account, does not contain advertising or analytics SDKs, and does not send your transaction data to a server operated by the developer.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how the DailyMint personal finance application ("DailyMint", "the app") handles information. DailyMint is developed by Ankit Kumar Mishra ("the developer").

2. Information handled by the app

Depending on the features you use, DailyMint may process the following information locally on your device:

  • Transaction details you enter, including names, amounts, dates, categories, and whether an amount represents spending, income, investment, or a personal share.
  • Bank transaction message content and sender information that you explicitly pass to DailyMint through an Apple Shortcuts automation. When a transaction is recognized, this source information is stored with the resulting ledger entry on your device. Messages that are ignored or cannot be recognized are not retained.
  • Your app preferences, including categories, tracking-cycle settings, theme, reminder settings, and automatic-import notification preference.

3. How the information is used

DailyMint uses this information only to provide features on your device, including:

  • Maintaining your personal transaction ledger.
  • Categorizing transactions and calculating summaries and growth views.
  • Importing bank transactions that match the automation you configure.
  • Recording only your portion of a shared expense when you choose to do so.
  • Scheduling optional reminders and notifying you about automatic imports.

4. Data collection, tracking, and sharing

The developer does not collect or receive your transaction data, message content, or preferences. DailyMint does not use a developer-operated backend service.

DailyMint does not:

  • Require or create a user account.
  • Use advertising, behavioral tracking, or third-party analytics.
  • Sell, rent, or share your personal or financial information with data brokers, advertisers, or other third parties.
  • Use your information to build an advertising profile.

Apple may process operational information related to downloading, purchasing, backing up, and using apps under Apple's own terms and privacy policy. That processing is controlled by Apple and is not data collected by DailyMint.

5. Permissions and integrations

Shortcuts: Automatic bank-message import on iPhone is optional. You choose which messages a Shortcuts automation passes to DailyMint. The app processes only the content supplied to its Import Bank SMS action.

Notifications: If you enable reminders or automatic-import alerts, DailyMint requests notification permission. Notifications are scheduled and delivered using Apple's notification services.

6. Storage and security

DailyMint stores its ledger, preferences, and recognized imported transaction information in the app's private storage on your device. The app uses platform storage protections and takes reasonable steps to avoid exposing this information to other apps.

Your device or app data may be included in backups managed by Apple, depending on your device and backup settings. Those backups are governed by your agreement with Apple. No method of electronic storage is guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Retention and deletion

Information remains on your device while it is needed for the app's features. You can delete individual transactions within DailyMint where that option is available. Deleting DailyMint removes its locally stored app data from the device, subject to any device backups retained by Apple under your backup settings.

Because the developer does not receive or maintain a server copy of your financial data, the developer cannot retrieve, export, or delete that data for you.

8. Children's privacy

DailyMint is a general personal finance tool and is not directed to children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if DailyMint's features or data practices change. The effective date and last-updated date at the top of this page will identify the current version. Material changes will be reflected in the app and its App Store disclosures when required.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the DailyMint privacy repository. Do not include transaction details, message content, account numbers, or other sensitive financial information in a public issue.
