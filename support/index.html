Support

Find setup guidance and answers to common DailyMint questions below.

Set up automatic bank SMS import

Open DailyMint Settings and select Open setup guide. The guide walks through creating an Apple Shortcuts automation that sends matching bank messages to DailyMint's Import Bank SMS action.

An automatic transaction did not appear

  1. Open Shortcuts and confirm that your message automation is enabled.
  2. Confirm that the automation passes the message as Shortcut Input to the DailyMint action.
  3. Check that the bank name entered in the automation matches the sender you expect.
  4. Run the automation again with a supported bank transaction message.

Messages that are not recognized as transactions are ignored and are not retained by DailyMint.

Notifications are not appearing

Check the reminder or automatic-import notification toggle in DailyMint Settings, then confirm that notifications are allowed for DailyMint in the iPhone Settings app.

Manage a transaction

Open Ledger and select a transaction to view its available actions. Depending on the transaction source, you can correct its classification, record your share of an expense, edit eligible fields, or delete the transaction.

Your data

DailyMint keeps its ledger and preferences in the app's private storage on your device. Read the DailyMint Privacy Policy for complete details.

Contact support

If the guidance above does not solve the problem, open a DailyMint support issue.

Include the DailyMint version, iOS version, device model, and steps to reproduce the problem. Do not include bank-message content, transaction details, account numbers, or other sensitive financial information because GitHub issues are public.
