Fend Your Spend

Support

Find setup guidance and answers to common Fend Your Spend questions below.

Set up automatic bank SMS import

Open Fend Your Spend Settings and select Open setup guide. The guide walks through creating an Apple Shortcuts automation that sends matching bank messages to Fend Your Spend's Import Bank SMS action.

An automatic transaction did not appear

  1. Open Shortcuts and confirm that your message automation is enabled.
  2. Confirm that the automation passes the message as Shortcut Input to the Fend Your Spend action.
  3. Check that the bank name entered in the automation matches the sender you expect.
  4. Run the automation again with a supported bank transaction message.

Messages that are not recognized as transactions are ignored and are not retained by Fend Your Spend.

Notifications are not appearing

Check the reminder or automatic-import notification toggle in Fend Your Spend Settings, then confirm that notifications are allowed for Fend Your Spend in the iPhone Settings app.

Manage a transaction

Open Ledger and select a transaction to view its available actions. Depending on the transaction source, you can correct its classification, record your share of an expense, edit eligible fields, or delete the transaction.

Your data

Fend Your Spend keeps its ledger and preferences in the app's private storage on your device. Read the Fend Your Spend Privacy Policy for complete details.

Contact support

If the guidance above does not solve the problem, open a Fend Your Spend support issue.

Include the Fend Your Spend version, iOS version, device model, and steps to reproduce the problem. Do not include bank-message content, transaction details, account numbers, or other sensitive financial information because GitHub issues are public.
